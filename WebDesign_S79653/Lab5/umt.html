About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It offers higher education in various fields of study, including science, technology, management and marine studies.

UMT is well known for its focus on marine science, aquatic resources and environmental studies. The university provides different academic programmes and research opportunities to help students develop their knowledge and skills.

UMT Faculties
